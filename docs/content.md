  - Project facts (from the team decks, 2026-11-25; Ziwen's own role still to confirm):
    - Check-in (NUS CDE5302 A3, team of 5): AI-assisted workspace for industrial design students. Research: interviews, immersion, observation; 57% repeat prompts because AI misreads intent. Pain: intent gets lost, can't judge manufacturability, polished AI output hides gaps. Solution: Keep / Explore / Constraints canvas, guided alternatives, manufacturing checks, reflection. Working prototype (Figma to Codex): https://check-in-prototype-design.vercel.app ; tested with 3 ID students.
    - Farmily (NUS CDE5311, team of 4): AI-assisted packaging design and marketplace connecting small farms with local shoppers. Research: 6 interviews, 35 survey responses, field visits, thematic analysis. Pain: farmers lack packaging/marketing reach; shoppers lack clear ingredient/allergen/origin info. Solution: AI brand + packaging drafts farmers approve, Farm Match, source-labelled answers with farmer handoff, AI-photo disclosure. Tested: 6 remote shopper sessions, 2 farmer interviews; 3 redesigns from findings.
    - OceanFeel (NUS x OceanX 2025, team of 5): haptic glove + touch-triggered audio on 3D-printed deep-sea creatures (sea cucumber) for visually impaired visitors. Research: interview with a visitor who lost his sight later in life. Built ESP32 glove (air chambers + vibration), tested with 5 blindfolded participants, iterated.
    - Within Reach (NUS CDE5301, team of 4, final 19 Nov 2026, "Timely help after a fall"): older adults living alone may lie on the floor for hours after a fall, and don't use alert devices (feel old, complex, false alarms). Research: 11 interviews (6 discovery, 5 validation), 2 field visits (care home, seniors' gym), 3 generations. Root cause: help only starts when they ask. Concept: stylish wristband (tilt + height-drop detection) + family app that routes calls to children, neighbours, then professionals; parent chooses what family sees. Next: 6-week wear trial (80% wear 5 of 7 days), bench test (95% detection). Concept wireframes, not yet tested.
    - MediaTek (NUS MT5920, team of 5, Ziwen = Strategy Designer): new market for MediaTek chips = Edge AI Sensor Hub for mining haul trucks, predicting failures offline. 57 interviews across mining, industry, investors and MediaTek. One mine loses ~$59M/yr to unplanned downtime (Siemens). Business case NPV $3.0B, ask $60M.
